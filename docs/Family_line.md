### Wietse-Botes-Details.md
- Full Name: Wietse (Wytsse/Wijtse/Wietse) Botes (progenitor/SV-PROG)
- Born: Before 24 April 1707, Enkhuizen, North Holland, Netherlands (primary sources align Enkhuizen origins)
- Baptized: 24 April 1707
- Parents: Son of Bote Lourens and Hylckje Wytzes
- Died: 21 July 1785, Swartland, Cape
- VOC Enlistment: ~1736-1737, Batavia service as ziekentrooster (repatriated 1739 with attestatie)
- Cape Arrival: 1744 on De Ruyter from Batavia, with wife Anna/Antje Pieterse and three surviving children
- Appointment: Ziekentrooster (comforter of the sick/voorleser/schoolmaster), Swartland (Malmesbury area) Dutch Reformed Church – served ~30 years, retired ~1774 (age ~67, role phased out)
- Side Roles: Koster (sexton) – dismissed 1753 upkeep issues; liquor sales conflicts (with Nicolaas von Wielich, prohibited 1766 incl Sundays, duty disruptions)
- Family: Married Anna/Antje Pieterse ~14 May 1730 (attestatie Leeuwarden/Goutum); children: Hijke (1731 died young), Hilletje/Hijke (1732 m. Johannes van der Westhuizen 1754), Boote (1735 Enkhuizen m. Susanna Margaretha Keuler 1768), Anthonie Abraham Adriaan (1740 Enkhuizen m. Engela Erasmus 1766 – main line carrier), Maria Anna (bapt 1746 Swartland Cape)
- Notes: Founded SA Botes line; overcame setbacks farming/family; children Cape melting pot adaptation (Portuguese locals); blended faith/education/community amid hardships
