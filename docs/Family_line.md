- Family: Married ~1730 Anna Pieterse; children include Hijke (1731 died young), Hijke (1732), Boote (1735), Anthonie Abraham Adriaan (~1740 Enkhuizen), Maria Anna (1746 Cape)
- Notes: Founded South African Botes line; minor church setbacks overcome through farming and family growth; kids learned Portuguese from Cape locals/slaves

## /Chapter-2-Pioneer-Grit
### Ryk-Jan-Botes-Details.md
- Full Name: Jan Jacobus "Ryk Jan" (JJ, Rijk Jan) Botes
- Born: ~1800 (variants Swellendam/Beaufort West/Cape Colony)
- Wealth Source: Livestock farming, transport wagons (ox-wagon freight)
- Farms: Sevenfontein, Beersheba/Barsheba (displaced by Moshoeshoe I tensions), Pietersberg, Botesberg (now part Amanzi Private Game Reserve)
- Appearance: White horsehair hat with black ribbon, otter pelt on journeys
- Contributions: Helped finance Twin-Towered Dutch Reformed Church (Tweelingkerk) Bloemfontein
- Political: Boomplaats Battle 1848 rebel – fined ~100-200 pounds; 1863 Orange Free State presidency candidacy (during M.W. Pretorius resignation period, J.H. Brand elected 1864)
- Marriage: Anna Maria Olivier OR Magdalena Adriana Francina Carolina Beukes (variants across sources)
- Family: Large Boer family; all but two sons (and possibly son-in-law) murdered with him
- Death: June 1865 (age ~65), near Brandfort/Salpeterspan farm, ambush en route to join commando at outset Second Free State–Basotho War (Seqiti War); Basotho raiders targeted wealth/livestock; Plaatjies settler group nearby also hit; possible German schoolmaster (Schwim?) involved/casualty

### Ryk-Jan-Botes-Stories.md
- Early scout/pre-Voortrekker pathfinder: Traveling horseman scouting bushveld Lesotho border age 23-28
- Frontier tragedy: Ambushed while mobilizing for war – wealth made him target; surviving sons carried line forward
- Yumpu family piece: "Ryk Jan Botes en die eerste voortrekkers" – early pioneer narrative
