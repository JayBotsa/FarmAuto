### Ryk-Jan-Details.md
- Full Name: Jan Jacobus "Ryk Jan" (JJ Botes, Rijk Jan) Botes
- Born: ~1800 (26 Aug 1800 variant) Cape Colony (Swellendam/Beaufort West/Koup district)
- Wealth: Livestock farming, transport wagons (ox-wagon freight bushveld Lesotho border)
- Farms: Sevenfontein, Beersheba/Barsheba (displaced by Moshoeshoe I tensions), Pietersberg, Botesberg (now part Amanzi Private Game Reserve)
- Appearance: White horsehair hat with black ribbon, otter pelt on journeys; rugged traveling horseman
- Contributions: Helped finance Twin-Towered Dutch Reformed Church (Tweelingkerk/Moederkerk) Bloemfontein (interest-free then 4.5%)
- Political: Boomplaats Battle 1848 rebel commando – fined ~100-200 pounds; 1863 Orange Free State presidency candidacy (M.W. Pretorius resignation period, J.H. Brand elected 1864)
- Marriage: Magdalena Adriana Francina Carolina Beukes (surviving widow probate); Anna Maria Olivier variant sources
- Family: Large Boer family (~12-14 children); most sons (incl Jan Jacobus, Barend Hendrik, Cornelis Johannes) and son-in-law murdered with him; survivors Hendrik Gerhardus & Anthonie Jacobus (separate brothers) escaped wounded w/ small child
